*Computational chemistry & artificial intelligence to advance drug discovery.*

I am a medicinal and computational chemist with a PhD in Cancer Sciences and over a decade of experience in oncology drug discovery. My career spans internationally recognised cancer research institutes, a global clinical-stage biotechnology company and a leading global contract research organisation (CRO), where I have developed expertise in small-molecule therapeutics, targeted protein degradation (PROTACs) and antibody–drug conjugates (ADCs).

My research interests lie at the intersection of **medicinal chemistry, computational drug discovery** and **emerging therapeutic technologies**. I am particularly interested in how molecular modelling, structure-based drug design and artificial intelligence can complement experimental approaches to accelerate the discovery of innovative medicines.

## Research interests

**Computational and structure-based drug design**

Molecular docking, protein–ligand interactions and virtual screening to guide compound design and lead optimisation.

**Targeted Protein Degradation (TPD)**

PROTACs, molecular glues and induced proximity therapeutics, including strategies to exploit alternative E3 ubiquitin ligases.

**AI-enabled drug discovery**

Computational workflows including machine learning, large language models and AI agents to support molecular design, property prediction and optimisation.
